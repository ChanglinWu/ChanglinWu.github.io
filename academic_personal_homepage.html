Biography & Overview

Section // 01

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Stanford Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (SAIL), working alongside Prof. Ellen Vance. Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at MIT, where I was advised by Prof. Marcus Sterling and supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.

My primary research interest lies at the intersection of Multimodal Machine Learning, Generative Representation Learning, and AI for Scientific Discovery. I aim to construct trustworthy foundational algorithms capable of reasoning seamlessly across diverse visual, textual, and structured biological modalities.

My work has received Best Paper Honorable Mention at NeurIPS 2024 and an Oral presentation at CVPR 2025. I am actively seeking collaborative interdisciplinary projects involving foundation models for chemistry and physics.
